Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of the information Ivy may process when you use the bot, why that information is needed, and how requests concerning it are handled.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

In short: Ivy processes only the information needed for the features enabled in a chat. Some moderation features require access to relevant messages and Telegram account or group information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Ivy, a third-party Telegram bot used for group management, moderation, security, and related features. Ivy is not operated by Telegram.

Telegram's own privacy practices are governed by Telegram's policies. This document describes how the Ivy service handles information that it receives through the Telegram Bot Platform.

2. Information Ivy May Process

The information processed depends on how Ivy is configured and which features are used.

Telegram account information

  • Telegram user ID
  • Username, when available
  • Display name and other information made available to the bot by Telegram

Group information

  • Chat or group ID
  • Group name
  • Administrator and permission information
  • Ivy configuration and moderation settings

Messages and moderation data

Where a feature requires it, Ivy may process messages, message metadata, warnings, moderation actions, filter matches, and temporary punishment information. For example, anti-spam, flood protection, link filtering, and content locks may require Ivy to inspect relevant messages.

3. How Information Is Used

Ivy uses information to operate the features requested by a group or user, including:

  • Moderation and administrator tools
  • Spam, flood, link, and raid protection
  • Warnings, mutes, restrictions, kicks, and bans
  • Filters and automatic moderation
  • Welcome and goodbye features
  • Group settings and notes
  • Statistics or XP features when enabled
  • Diagnostics, security, and service administration

Ivy does not intentionally use collected information for unrelated profiling or advertising purposes.

4. Storage and Retention

Necessary configuration and moderation data may be stored so that Ivy can preserve settings and features across restarts.

Examples can include user IDs, group IDs, settings, warnings, moderation history, filters, notes, and feature-specific records.

Information should be retained only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the service, security, or applicable legal requirements. Actual retention periods may depend on the implementation of the Ivy service.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

Ivy does not sell or rent personal information.

Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the service, protect its security, comply with a valid legal obligation, or where disclosure is otherwise authorized by the person or organization responsible for the data.

If third-party infrastructure or services are used to operate Ivy, information may be processed by those providers only as necessary for the service and subject to the applicable arrangements and laws.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled by Ivy against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid sending sensitive personal information to a bot unless it is necessary for the feature being used.

7. Data Deletion Requests

You may contact the Ivy operator to request deletion of personal information stored by the service, where applicable.

After a valid request, information that is no longer required for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes should be deleted within a reasonable period.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for security, or contained in backups until those backups are replaced or expire according to the applicable retention process.

8. Telegram

Ivy is an independent third-party bot and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Telegram.

Telegram may independently process information according to its own policies and terms.
